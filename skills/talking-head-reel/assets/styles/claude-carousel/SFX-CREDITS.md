Freesound originals need a login; the HQ preview (128 kbps mp3, `cdn.freesound.org/previews/<id>-hq.mp3`) is plenty for <1 s effects. RMS-match clips with `volumedetect` (loudnorm on sub-second clips outputs silence). Use them sparingly: about one per scene, volume ≈0.25.
